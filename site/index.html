    How to read this

    • An open station means this packet has not established that stage. It does not mean the work was never done.
    • Each stage needs its own evidence. Authorised funding is not a contract, and a contract is not completed work.
    • Amounts are shown by year as published. They are not totalled across different projects or stages.
    • No record claims that any street or home is protected.

    Sources to connect next

    • GVA Oberta DANA section: aid, contract and donation viewers, plus a local transparency space joined by 53 town councils.
    • Diputació de València DANA portal: contracts by service, decrees and its emergency protocol.
    • Central government DANA data updates: periodic PDF reports on aid and works.
    • Public-sector contracts platform and the BOE: award and expropriation notices to confirm contract and progress stages.
